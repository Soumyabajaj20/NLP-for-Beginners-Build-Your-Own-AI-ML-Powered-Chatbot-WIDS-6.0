# 🤖 NLP for Beginners: Build Your Own AI/ML-Powered Chatbot
### WiDS 6.0 (Winter in Data Science) · Guided Project · 5 Weeks

> Ever wondered how ChatGPT understands you, how Gmail catches spam, or how Amazon knows a review is negative?
> In 5 weeks, you'll go from writing your **very first line of Python** to building **your own AI chatbot** that answers questions from your own notes.

**No prior coding or ML experience needed.** If you have a laptop, a Google account and some curiosity, you're ready.

---

## 📌 About the Project

Natural Language Processing (NLP) is the part of AI that teaches computers to read, understand and generate human language. It powers ChatGPT, Google Translate, spam filters, autocomplete, voice assistants and much more.

In this project you will:
1. Learn **Python from scratch**, with a focus on working with text
2. Turn text into numbers and build your first **ML classifiers** (spam detector, sentiment analyser)
3. Understand **word embeddings** and get an intuitive feel for **Transformers**, the architecture behind every modern LLM
4. Use **Hugging Face** models and **LLM APIs**
5. Build a **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) chatbot** with a simple web UI as your final project

## 🗓️ Weekly Plan

| Week | Topic | What you'll build |
|------|-------|-------------------|
| [Week 1](Week_1_Python_Basics/README.md) | **Python from Zero**: variables, strings, loops, lists, dictionaries, functions, intro to pandas | A text statistics analyser + a rule-based sentiment checker |
| Week 2 | **Text as Data**: cleaning, tokenization, bag-of-words, TF-IDF, first ML classifier | Spam detector + movie review sentiment analyser |
| Midterm | Text classification mini-project | Your own classifier on a dataset of your choice |
| Week 3 | **Words as Vectors**: word embeddings, similarity, semantic search, how Transformers work (intuition) | A semantic search engine |
| Week 4 | **Hugging Face & LLM APIs**: pretrained pipelines, prompt engineering, chatbots | A simple chatbot |
| Week 5 | **Final Project**: RAG chatbot with Streamlit | A chatbot that answers questions from your own documents |

*Weeks 2–5 will be released progressively.*

## 🛠️ Setup (2 minutes, nothing to install!)

We use **Google Colab**, a free Python notebook that runs in your browser.

1. Make sure you're signed in to your Google account.
2. Click any **"Open in Colab"** badge in this repo.
3. In Colab, click **File → Save a copy in Drive** so you can edit your own copy.
4. Run a cell with **Shift + Enter**.

New to Colab? Watch: [Colab 101: Beginner's Guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ii6gs9zADEA)
